Website Portal Guide

1Signing in and finding your way around

  1. Go to themarginco.agency/admin/login and enter your email and password.
  2. You land in the portal. The menu on the left has everything: Pages, SEO, Site settings, Blog, Case Studies, Video Testimonials, Leads, Team. The menu scrolls if your screen is short.
  3. Your email and role are shown at the bottom of the menu. Sign out is always at the top right.
  4. View website (in the menu) opens the public site in a new tab.
RoleWhat it can do
AdminEverything: edit content, publish, blog, case studies, videos, leads, team.
SEO editorOnly search-engine settings (titles, descriptions, keywords). Cannot change page content or see leads.
Golden rule: everything you change is saved as a draft first. Visitors only see it after you press Publish.

2Editing text and images directly on the page

This is the easiest way to change any page (Home, About, Contact, the three Services pages, Blog and Case Studies pages) because you edit exactly what visitors see.

Start editing

  1. Sign in, then open the page you want on the normal website (for example the Home page).
  2. Click the Edit this page button that appears on the page. (You can also open Pages in the portal and choose Edit on site.)
  3. An edit bar appears at the bottom of the screen: Discard Save draft Publish Exit, plus the page's SEO score.

Change text

Click any heading or paragraph and type. It is plain text, so there is no formatting to worry about.

Change an image

Click the image, choose a new one (JPG, PNG or WebP, max 5 MB) and fill in its alt text: a short description of what the picture shows. It helps accessibility and Google.

Add, remove or reorder items, and hide sections

Each section has a small edit control. It opens a side panel where you can change lists (services, FAQs, testimonials, steps), links, icons, and switch a whole section off (hidden from visitors) or back on.

Save and publish

  • Save draft keeps your work safely without showing it to visitors.
  • Publish makes it live (the site updates within a few minutes).
  • Discard throws away unpublished changes.
  • Exit leaves edit mode. You will be asked first if there are unsaved edits.
Careful: the header, footer and contact details are shared by every page. Changing them here changes them everywhere.

3Editing pages from the portal (and undoing changes)

Prefer forms to clicking on the page? Open Pages in the menu and click a page. Each page has four tabs:

TabUse it for
ContentAll the words, images and links on that page, in a form.
SEOThe title and description shown in Google, plus the social sharing card.
SchemaExtra search-engine data (advanced: usually leave as is).
HistoryEvery published version. Pick an old one and choose Restore to bring it back as a draft, then Publish.
Made a mistake after publishing? Open the page's History tab, restore the last good version and publish it again.

4Site settings: email, Calendly link, menu, footer

Open Site settings (Admin role). This is where site-wide details live:

  • Contact email: shown on the Contact page and footer. Please replace the placeholder with your real address.
  • Booking link (Calendly): used by every "book a call" button across the site.
  • Brand name, tagline, navigation menu, footer text and the yellow call-to-action band.

Edit the fields, save, then publish. Changes appear on every page.

5Publishing a blog post

  1. Menu → Blog → New post.
  2. Fill in the Title. The web address (URL slug) is created for you; you can edit it.
  3. Write a short Excerpt (10 to 300 characters). It appears on the blog list and in shares.
  4. Choose a Category.
  5. Write the article in the editor. The toolbar has headings, bold, italic, lists, links, images, colours and more. What you see is how it will look on the site.
  6. Add a Featured image (JPG, PNG, WebP; max 5 MB; about 1200×630 looks best) and its alt text.
  7. Open the SEO panel: Meta title (aim for under 60 characters), Meta description (about 150 to 160), keywords and a focus keyword.
  8. Check the Publishing checklist on the right, then press Publish now. Use Save draft if it is not ready.

Later changes: open All posts, click a post, edit, then Update post. Use Unpublish to draft to take it offline without deleting it. The list has tabs for All / Published / Drafts and a search box.

Writing tip: one clear idea per post, short paragraphs, and headings every few paragraphs. Add a link to /contact at the end.

6Publishing a case study

Case studies work like blog posts, with a few extras. Menu → Case Studies → New case study.

  • Title, URL slug, Excerpt, Industry (required), Channels (optional, e.g. "Meta Ads · Facebook Ads").
  • Metrics: click Add metric to show headline results as badges (up to 6). Each has a value (e.g. "3.2x", max 12 characters), a label (e.g. "ROAS") and an optional short detail.
  • The story itself goes in the editor: the problem, what you did, the results.
  • Featured image with alt text, the SEO panel, then Publish now.

The three most recent published case studies appear automatically on the Home page. All of them appear on the Case Studies page.

Only publish real results. Use numbers and client names you have permission to share. Vague or invented claims hurt trust.

7Adding a video testimonial

Menu → Video Testimonials → New video. Videos are stored securely on your own website (no YouTube needed) and appear on the Home page after the "Growth Architecture" section.

  1. Enter the client's Name. Role and Company are optional but look more credible.
  2. Drag the video file into the upload box (or click to choose it). It must be an MP4 (WebM also works), up to 50 MB. Wait for the upload to finish and the preview to appear.
  3. Add a Poster image, the picture shown before someone presses play. Either click use a frame from the video or upload your own. Add short alt text.
  4. Set the Sort order (lower numbers show first).
  5. Press Publish now.
Video too big? Free tools such as HandBrake (choose "Fast 720p30") shrink a phone video a lot without visible loss. WhatsApp videos are usually already small enough.

Visitors only download the video when they press play, so it does not slow the page down. To remove one, open it and choose Unpublish to draft or delete it.

8Reading your enquiries (Leads)

Everything sent through the website's contact form is collected in Leads (Admin role only).

  • Each row shows name, email, the service they chose, their message and the date.
  • Click Mark handled once you have replied. The tabs at the top separate New from Handled.
  • The trash icon deletes an enquiry (you will be asked to confirm).

The form has built-in spam protection and limits repeated submissions.

Check Leads regularly. The portal does not email you when a new enquiry arrives. Reply within one business day, as the website promises.

9SEO tips and quick fixes

  • SEO in the menu shows a score for every page and highlights what to fix (missing descriptions, titles too long, and so on).
  • Page title: under about 60 characters. Description: about 150 to 160. Both should read like a human wrote them and include the main topic.
  • Always add alt text to images.
  • The sitemap (themarginco.agency/sitemap.xml) updates by itself when you publish. Submit it once in Google Search Console.
